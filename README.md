# Customer-Retention-Analytics
Analyzed customer churn patterns and retention risks using Python, SQL, and Excel to generate actionable business insights.

📈 **Customer Retention Analytics**

📌 **Project Overview**

A data analytics project focused on understanding customer churn patterns and identifying customer retention risks using Python, SQL, and Excel. The project transforms raw customer data into meaningful insights to help businesses understand customer behavior and improve retention strategies.

🎯 **Project Objectives**

Analyze customer churn and retention patterns.

Identify factors associated with customer attrition.

Segment customers based on retention risk.

Discover trends and patterns in customer behavior.

Support data-driven business decisions.

🛠️ **Tools & Technologies**

Python – Data analysis and visualization.

Pandas – Data cleaning and transformation.

SQL – Data querying and analysis.

Excel – Data exploration and reporting.

🔍 **Key Activities**

Performed exploratory data analysis (EDA).

Cleaned and transformed raw customer datasets.

Analyzed customer churn patterns and retention risks.

Segmented customers based on retention behavior.

Created visualizations to communicate analytical findings.

💡 **Project Outcome**

Identified meaningful customer retention patterns and business insights through data analysis, helping demonstrate how analytics can support customer retention and business decision-making.
